## 🌟 Overview & Key Offerings

**Janatha Sarees** is Soraba’s cherished family retail textile destination, located on Car Street (Ratha Beedi) near the Old Bus Stand. This web app delivers a fast, modern e-commerce storefront inspired by leading shopping apps like **Myntra** and **Ajio**, blending traditional Malnad hospitality with contemporary, thumb-friendly retail commerce.

- **Sarees Collection**: Bridal Kanjeevarams, Pure Banarasi, Soft Silks, Authentic Ilkal handlooms, Daily Wear Handloom Cottons, Floral Chiffons, Georgettes, and trending Organza.
- **Men's Ethnic & Formals**: Traditional Silk Panche (Dhoti) with Zari borders, matching Shalyas, Raw Silk Wedding Kurtas, Nehru Jackets, and Formal Shirts.
- **Kids' Festive Couture**: Traditional Pattu Pavada / Langas for girls, boys' Silk Kurta-Dhoti sets, party frocks, and festive ensembles.
- **Occasions & Events**: Comprehensive wardrobes for Marriages (Muhurtham), Gruha Pravesha (Housewarmings), Diwali, Ugadi, and affordable Taamboola return-gift saree packages.
- **Daily Comfort & Sportswear**: 100% pure cotton nighties, stylish loungewear pajama coordinates, and high-stretch activewear/gym tracks.
- **Honest Pricing Policy**: Direct-from-weavers affordable retail & wholesale value. No artificial or inflated price tags — customers inquire directly on WhatsApp or visit the showroom for personalized recommendations, color choices, and best daily rates.

## 🛍️ Trending Myntra & Ajio E-Commerce Experience

The storefront has been designed around modern app conventions:

1. **App Header with Sticky Live Search**:
   - Sticky header with JS emblem, brand title, live global search input, Wishlist badge counter, Showroom directions, and WhatsApp quick-pill.
   - Live real-time search instantly filters product cards across both desktop and mobile viewports.

2. **Instagram / Myntra Story Circles Rail**:
   - Colorful animated gradient rings showcasing key store departments:
     - 🥻 `Sarees`
     - 👑 `Men's Wear`
     - 👧 `Kids Wear`
     - 💍 `Muhurtham`
     - 🌙 `Comfort & Active`
     - ✨ `Trends`
     - 📍 `Showroom`
   - Tapping any story instantly filters the product catalog with silky smooth animations.

3. **3:4 E-Commerce Product Cards**:
   - Standard high-fashion 3:4 aspect ratio cards.
   - Clean floating rating chips (`★ 4.9`).
   - Interactive heart wishlist toggle with instantaneous badge counter updates.
   - Value guarantee badges (`Direct Weaver Value / ಮಗ್ಗದ ನೇರ ಬೆಲೆ`).
   - 1-tap WhatsApp direct order button with pre-formatted inquiry text.

4. **Slide-Over Wishlist Drawer**:
   - Slide-in side drawer displaying saved looks with thumbnail previews.
   - "Share Wishlist on WhatsApp" button compiles the user's checklist into a single message to send to the store before their visit.

5. **Ajio Trends & Lookbook Reels**:
   - Modern 9:14 vertical reels displaying trending drape inspirations (Lilac Organza, Olive Kurta Jackets, Bridal Kanjeevarams).

6. **Mobile Bottom App Navigation Dock**:
   - Persistent bottom dock featuring **Home**, **Trends**, **Wishlist**, and **Chat** buttons for comfortable one-thumb navigation.

7. **Bilingual Engine (`EN | ಕನ್ನಡ`)**:
   - Instant 1-tap toggle between English and Kannada across headers, product cards, badges, and FAQs.
